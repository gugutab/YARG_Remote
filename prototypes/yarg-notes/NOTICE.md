# The 3D note models and textures in this folder

`notes.json`, `NormalNote.png` and `CymbalNote.png` come from YARG (https://github.com/YARC-Official/YARG), the files
`Assets/Art/Meshes/Gameplay/Notes/Rectangular/*.fbx` (converted by `scripts/pack-note-meshes.mjs`) and
`Assets/Art/Textures/Gameplay/Notes/Rectangular/{NormalNote,CymbalNote}.png`.
